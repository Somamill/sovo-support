# Small App Review sample

`alice-preview.m4b` is a two-minute excerpt from the first chapter of *Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland*, Lewis Carroll, read by Kara Shallenberg for LibriVox (catalogued 18 March 2010). It is an import/playback sample, not the complete audiobook, and is not bundled in any app target.

Source and narrator credits: https://librivox.org/alices-adventures-in-wonderland-by-lewis-carroll-4/
Original M4B: https://archive.org/download/alices_adventures_1003/AlicesAdventuresInWonderlandVer4_librivox.m4b
Source license record: https://archive.org/metadata/alices_adventures_1003

LibriVox identifies its recordings as public domain in the USA and asks listeners elsewhere to verify local copyright status. The source metadata links the Creative Commons public-domain dedication. No commercial cover or third-party portrait is included. The original AAC audio is copied without re-encoding, with a single correctly bounded chapter and fresh bibliographic tags. User/device metadata is not copied.

Download the sample to Files, then use sovo. → Library → + → Import audio files. Tap the imported preview to play. The existing complete-classic download remains optional and may be slower.
